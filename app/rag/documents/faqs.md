# Bookly FAQs

## What is Bookly?
Bookly is an AI-powered appointment booking assistant designed to make salon and wellness booking simple and personalized. It helps customers discover salons, explore services, and book appointments through a chat-first interface. Bookly connects users with trusted local salons, helps them find services that fit their needs, and streamlines the entire booking process.

## What is Bookly's mission?
Bookly's mission is to make salon and wellness appointment booking simple, personalized, and accessible for every customer. We connect users with trusted local salons, help them discover services that fit their needs, and reduce friction across booking, payments, and cancellations.

## How do I book an appointment?
To book an appointment through Bookly:
1. Open the Bookly chat assistant or mobile app
2. Select a salon from the available list
3. Choose a service you want (haircut, massage, nails, etc.)
4. Pick your preferred date and time from available slots
5. Enter your contact information and appointment details
6. Confirm your booking and receive a confirmation number
You can also browse salon profiles, view staff expertise, and read customer reviews before booking.

## Can I cancel an appointment?
Yes, you can cancel an appointment through Bookly. The ability to cancel depends on your salon's cancellation policy:
- Most salons allow cancellation at least 24 hours before the appointment
- Some salons have a 48-hour cancellation policy for better planning
- Cancellations made within 5 hours of the appointment may incur a cancellation fee or result in no refund
- Late cancellations (less than 2 hours) typically forfeit the full appointment fee
To cancel, go to your Bookly app, find the appointment, and click "Request Cancellation". The salon will confirm the cancellation and any applicable fees.

## What happens if I cancel 5 hours before my appointment?
If you cancel 5 hours before your appointment, you may be charged a late cancellation fee. Most Bookly salons consider cancellations within 5 hours as late cancellations. Depending on the salon's policy, you may:
- Lose 50% of the appointment fee as a cancellation penalty
- Be charged the full appointment fee
- Still receive a partial refund if the salon allows it
The exact terms depend on the specific salon's cancellation policy. You can check the salon's cancellation terms in the appointment details before booking.

## How is a salon recommendation generated?
Bookly recommendations are generated based on multiple factors:
- Service fit: We prioritize salons that offer the exact service you're looking for
- Location: Salons closer to your current location are ranked higher
- Rating and reviews: Higher-rated salons with positive customer reviews are recommended first
- Staff expertise: Salons with stylists/professionals specialized in your requested service are prioritized
- Availability: We show salons with nearby available appointment slots
- Price range: Recommendations match your budget preferences where possible

## Can I get a refund after my appointment?
Refunds depend on your salon's refund policy and cancellation terms:
- If you cancel before the cancellation window closes, you're eligible for a full refund according to the salon's refund policy
- If you cancel after the window or miss the appointment, refund eligibility depends on the salon's refund policy
- Completed services are generally non-refundable unless there was a quality issue
- To request a refund, contact the salon directly through your Bookly appointment or use the "Request Refund" option in the app
- Refunds are typically processed within 5-7 business days back to your original payment method

## What is the Bookly service fee?
Bookly charges the following service fees:
- Booking through the Bookly platform: 2% service fee (charged to the customer or covered by salon based on their agreement)
- Payment processing: Credit card and digital wallet transactions have a 2.9% + $0.30 processing fee
- Cancellation handling: No additional fees for requesting cancellations through Bookly
- Salon commission: Salons using Bookly Pro pay 5% commission on booked appointments
Note: Specific fees may vary by region and subscription plan. Check with your salon for their exact pricing and fee structure.
