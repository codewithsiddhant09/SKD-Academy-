# Bookly Policies

## Cancellation Policy
Bookly enables each salon to set their own cancellation policy. Here are the common cancellation terms:

**Standard Cancellation Terms:**
- Cancellations made 24 hours or more before the appointment: Full refund or credit
- Cancellations made 5-24 hours before: 50% refund or cancellation fee may apply
- Cancellations made less than 5 hours before: Late cancellation fee applies (typically 25-100% of appointment cost)
- No-shows (missed appointments without cancellation): Full appointment fee charged, no refund

**How Cancellation Works:**
1. Go to your Bookly appointment in the app or chat
2. Click "Request Cancellation" and provide a reason (optional)
3. The salon receives your cancellation request and confirms within 1-2 hours
4. If approved, your appointment is removed from the calendar
5. Refunds or credits are applied according to the salon's policy

**Important Notes:**
- Different salons may have different cancellation windows (24 hours, 48 hours, or custom)
- Peak times (weekends, holidays) may have stricter cancellation policies
- Emergency/medical cancellations may be subject to waived fees at salon discretion
- Always check the salon's specific cancellation terms before booking

## Refund Policy
Refunds are processed according to appointment type and timing:

**Appointment Cancellations:**
- Cancellations within the allowed window: Full refund or service credit
- Late cancellations (within 5 hours): 50% refund or full fee charged per salon policy
- No-shows: No refund, full appointment fee retained by salon

**Completed Services:**
- Services must be completed satisfactorily for no refund
- Quality issues: Contact salon within 24 hours for dispute resolution
- If unresolved, Bookly can mediate refund requests

**Refund Processing:**
- Refunds are processed to the original payment method
- Credit card refunds: 3-5 business days
- Digital wallet (Apple Pay, Google Pay): 1-2 business days
- Bookly account credit: Immediate
- Most refunds appear within 7 business days

## Payment Methods
Bookly supports the following payment methods:
- Credit cards (Visa, Mastercard, American Express)
- Debit cards
- Digital wallets such as Apple Pay and Google Pay
- Salon-specific gift cards and credits where accepted
- In-app stored payment methods saved securely for quick checkout

**Payment Notes:**
- All payments are processed securely through encrypted payment gateways.
- The exact payment methods available may vary by salon and region.
- You can change or remove stored payment methods in your Bookly account settings.

**Refund Limitations:**
- Non-refundable services must be clearly marked before booking
- Salon can retain fees for cancellations within their policy window
- Refunds cannot exceed the original appointment amount

## Privacy Policy
Bookly's privacy policy commits to protecting your personal customer data:

**Data We Collect:**
Bookly collects and processes the following customer data:
- Personal information: Name, phone number, email, address
- Appointment history: Services, dates, salons, preferences
- Payment information: Processed securely through encrypted payment gateways
- Usage data: App interactions, search history, preferences
- Device information: Device ID, OS type, app version (for support and analytics)

**How We Use Your Data:**
- Appointment booking and management
- Sending appointment confirmations, reminders, and updates
- Processing payments securely
- Improving Bookly services and user experience
- Customer support and dispute resolution
- Analytics and fraud prevention
- Marketing communications (with your consent)

**Data Protection:**
- All data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS/TLS)
- Sensitive payment data is tokenized and never stored on Bookly servers
- Access to personal data is restricted to authorized staff only
- We comply with GDPR, CCPA, and local data protection regulations
- Data backups are secured and protected

**Your Rights:**
- You can view your personal information anytime in your Bookly profile
- Request data correction or deletion (subject to legal requirements)
- Opt-out of marketing emails anytime
- Download your appointment history and personal data
- We will never sell or share your personal data with third parties

**Data Retention:**
- Active appointment data: Retained while account is active
- Completed appointments: Retained for 2 years for records
- Payment data: Deleted per PCI compliance (immediate or encrypted)
- Opt-out data: Retained to honor your preferences (1 year)

**Contact Us:**
For privacy questions or data requests, email privacy@bookly.com or use the privacy request form in your account settings.
