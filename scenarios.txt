Bookly – Agentic AI Chatbot Scenarios
Scenario 1: Smart Recommendation Based on Previous Bookings
Description
When a user requests to view or book a service they have previously booked, the AI
analyses their booking history and recommends the most suitable salon.
Recommendation Priority
The AI recommends salons based on the following priority:
1. Highest Rated Salon by the User
○ If the user has rated one or more salons for the requested service, the
salon with the highest rating is recommended first.
2. Most Frequently Booked Salon
○ If no ratings are available, the AI recommends the salon where the user
has booked the same service the most.
3. Most Recent Booking
○ If multiple salons have the same booking count, the AI recommends the
salon where the user most recently booked the service.
Example Conversation
User:
Show me all my previous haircut bookings.
AI:
You have booked the Haircut service four times.
Previous Bookings:
● Salon A – 2 Bookings – Rated 5 Stars
● Salon B – 2 Bookings – No Rating
Based on your previous experience, I recommend Salon A because you have rated it
the highest.
Would you like to book your next haircut with Salon A?
User:
Yes.
AI:
Your preferred salon has been selected. Please provide your preferred appointment
date and time.

Scenario 4: Individual and Group Booking Detection
Description
The AI automatically determines whether the booking is for an individual or a group
by analyzing the user's message.
Individual Booking
If the message refers only to the user, such as:
● Book a facial for me.
● Book my haircut.
The AI treats the booking as an individual appointment.
Group Booking
If the message includes another person, such as:
● Book for me and my sister.
● Book for me and my mother.
● Book for me and Sarah.
The AI treats the request as a group booking.
Example Conversation
User:
Book a manicure for me and my sister this Saturday.
AI:
I understand this is a group booking for two people.
Please provide your preferred appointment time.
User:
5:00 PM.
AI:
I am searching for salons that can accommodate both appointments together.
I found Bella Ladies Salon with availability for two people at 5:00 PM.
Would you like me to submit the booking request?

Why Payment Should Be Triggered Through the Application Instead of
the Chat?
Reason 1 Bookings Are Business Transactions, Not Chat Conversations
AI conversations are temporary by nature. Users may end a conversation, start a new
one, clear their chat history, or even uninstall and reinstall the application. However, a
booking is an ongoing process that should continue until it reaches its final outcome,
regardless of whether the original AI conversation still exists.
Example Scenario
29 June
The user starts a conversation with the AI.
User:
I want to book a facial for 1 July at 5:00 PM.
The AI:
● Collects the booking details.
● Searches for suitable salons.
● Recommends the best option.
● Submits the booking request to the selected salon.
The conversation then ends.
30 June
The user opens Bookly and starts a completely new AI conversation. The previous
chat may no longer be visible or may have been archived.
At 2:00 PM, the salon accepts the booking request.
Since the original AI conversation is no longer active, the booking process should not
depend on that conversation to continue.
Instead, the Bookly backend immediately sends an in-app notification:
"Your booking for 1 July at Bella Ladies Salon has been accepted. Please
complete your payment within the next 10 minutes to confirm your
appointment."
When the user taps the notification:
1. The Bookly application opens.
2. 3. 4. 5. The booking details are displayed.
The user taps Pay Now.
The integrated Telr payment flow is launched.
After successful payment, the booking is confirmed.
Why is this approach recommended?
A booking has its own lifecycle that continues independently of the chat. Once a
booking request has been created, it moves through several stages until it is
completed.
For example, a booking may progress through the following stages:
● Pending
● Accepted
● Rejected
● Payment Pending
● Confirmed
● Cancelled
● Expired
These events are managed by the Bookly platform and should continue even if the
user is no longer viewing the AI conversation.
Separating the booking workflow from the chat ensures that users never miss
important updates such as salon acceptance, payment requests, booking
confirmations, or cancellations. It also creates a more reliable and production-ready
booking experience.
Reason 2: Payment Gateway SDK Integration
Payment gateways such as Telr provide a Mobile SDK that is integrated into the
Bookly mobile application, not into the AI chat interface.
Note: The SDK is the component that displays the secure payment screen and
processes the customer's payment within the application.
Why can't the payment SDK run inside the chat?
The AI chat is a conversational interface. It can:
● Display messages.
● Present booking details.
● Show buttons.
● Collect user responses.
However, it cannot host or execute a payment gateway SDK.
The Telr Mobile SDK requires access to native mobile capabilities such as:
● Secure payment screens
● Device security features
● Native Android and iOS APIs
● Application lifecycle callbacks
● Secure communication with the Telr payment platform
These capabilities are available only within the mobile application and cannot be
executed inside a chat conversation.
Reason 3: Security and Customer Trust
One of the most important aspects of any booking platform is maintaining customer
trust during the payment process.
Customers are naturally more cautious when making online payments. A dedicated
payment screen within the Bookly application provides confidence by clearly
displaying:
● Booking details
● Salon information
● Appointment date and time
● Total payable amount
before redirecting the user to the secure Telr payment gateway.
Separating the payment process from the chat also reduces the possibility of:
● Confusion regarding payment requests
● Expired payment links
● Suspicion about the authenticity of payment messages
● Potential phishing concerns
A structured payment flow within the application creates a more professional and
trustworthy user experience.
Reason 4: Maintaining a Seamless User Experience
If the AI chatbot sends a payment link that opens an external browser, the user is
temporarily taken outside the Bookly application.
Although the payment itself remains secure, this interruption can negatively impact
the overall booking experience and may reduce payment completion rates.
A smoother approach is to initiate payment directly from within the Bookly application
using the integrated Telr payment flow.
This keeps the user within the application throughout the booking journey, resulting
in:
● A more consistent experience
● Higher user confidence
● Better payment completion rates
● Reduced risk of users abandoning the payment process
Why We Do Not Recommend Storing Complete AI Chat or User
Conversations History?
Reason 1: AI Context Window
Modern AI models operate using a context window, which represents the amount of
information the AI reads before generating each response.
Before the AI answers any question, it looks at the information available to understand
the user's request and provide a relevant response.
This information may include:
● Recent conversations
● Previous bookings
● Favourite salons
● Preferred services
● Saved user preferences
The more unnecessary conversation history the AI has to read, the longer it takes to
understand the request and generate a response.
Example
Imagine a user has had 500 conversations over the past year.
Today the user simply asks:
"Book me a facial tomorrow."
The AI does not need to read:
● Yesterday's conversation about birthday makeup
● Last month's discussion about manicure prices
● A six-month-old conversation about hair colouring
● Hundreds of greetings and casual messages
Instead, the AI only needs information that is useful for the current request, such as:
● Preferred salon
● Previous facial bookings
● Preferred location
● Preferred appointment time
● Favourite services
By using only relevant information, the AI can respond much faster and provide more
accurate recommendations.
Reason 2: Better AI Performance
Each AI response is generated using the available conversation context.
The larger the conversation history, the more information the AI must process before
producing a response.
This can result in:
● Increased response time
● Higher AI processing costs
● Greater likelihood of irrelevant information influencing the response
Example
User: Book a facial.
If the AI must first process hundreds of previous messages, the response will be
slower and potentially less focused than if it simply uses the user's booking history
and saved preferences.
Using structured business data instead of a complete conversation history
significantly improves both speed and accuracy.
Reason 3: Increased Risk of Errors Due to Large AI Context
AI models have a limit on the amount of information they can process in a single
request. If every conversation is continuously stored and sent to the AI, the context
gradually becomes larger over time.
As the context grows, the likelihood of errors also increases. The AI may become
confused by outdated or irrelevant conversations, resulting in inaccurate responses or
recommendations. In cases where the total context exceeds the AI model's maximum
processing limit, the AI may fail to generate a response altogether and return an error
indicating that the request is too large to process.
Reason 4: Lower Infrastructure Costs
Every stored conversation requires ongoing resources, including:
● Database storage
● Secure data management
● Retrieval during AI processing
● Backup and maintenance
With thousands of active users, AI conversations can quickly grow into millions of
stored messages.
A large proportion of these conversations provide little or no long-term value.
For example, messages such as:
● "Hello"
● "Thank you"
● "Good morning"
● "Can you help me?"
do not contribute to future recommendations or booking decisions.
Recommended Approach
Instead of storing every conversation indefinitely, Bookly should retain structured
business information that genuinely improves the user experience.
This includes:
● Booking history
● Completed services
● Favourite salons
● User ratings
● Preferred appointment times
● Preferred locations
● Frequently booked services
● Loyalty points
● Saved preferences
These records allow the AI to deliver highly personalised recommendations without
processing unnecessary conversation history.
Bookly should retain structured business information (such as bookings, ratings, and
preferences) because this helps the AI deliver personalised recommendations and
faster responses.
